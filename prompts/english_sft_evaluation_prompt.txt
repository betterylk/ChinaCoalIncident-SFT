[Judge model]
Use GPT-5.6 Sol for all 54 pairwise case evaluations.

[Evaluator role]
Act as an expert evaluator of coal mine accident analysis responses.

[Input structure]
Each case contains case_id, system, user, assistant_before_sft, and
assistant_after_sft. The fields represent the incident identifier, task
instruction and constraints, accident timeline, response before SFT, and
response after SFT, respectively.

[Evaluation principle]
Evaluate assistant_before_sft and assistant_after_sft independently
against the task instruction and provided accident evidence. Do not assume
that the after-SFT response is better.

[Scoring]
Score each response from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent) on each evaluation
dimension.

[Instruction following]
Evaluate adherence to the requested task and constraints.

[Factual grounding]
Evaluate consistency with the provided accident evidence and penalize
unsupported or fabricated facts.

[Accident mechanism]
Evaluate the accuracy of the explanation of how the accident developed.

[Root-cause analysis]
Evaluate the identification of direct and underlying causes supported by
the provided evidence.

[Preventive recommendations]
Evaluate the relevance, specificity, and actionability of the proposed
preventive recommendations.

[Clarity/conciseness]
Evaluate clarity, organization, and absence of unnecessary redundancy.

[Overall quality]
Evaluate holistic correctness, reasoning quality, and usefulness.

[Pairwise comparison]
After independently scoring both responses, select the pairwise winner as
before, after, or tie, and provide a concise case-specific rationale.
