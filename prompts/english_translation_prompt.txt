[Input unit]
Translate exactly one accepted Chinese JSONL record at a time; do not
construct a new incident from the official report during this stage.

[Identifier control]
Copy incident_id and province_case_id exactly. Do not renumber, reorder,
merge, or split incidents.

[Structural control]
Preserve the JSON hierarchy, field names, three-message role order, array
membership, null values, dates, times, numerical counts, and
official-report URL.

[Text translation]
Translate the task, message content, overview, equipment labels, accident
categories, mine and location names, personnel descriptors, and source
descriptors into clear English. Preserve the chronology and the separation
among mechanism, direct cause, contributing factors, and recommendations.

[Grounding]
Do not add, omit, infer, or strengthen accident facts, causal findings,
casualties, or recommendations. Resolve ambiguity by consulting the
Chinese master record and its official source rather than inventing
English content.

[Quality control]
Compare the Chinese and English records for matching identifiers, event
order and count, numerical attributes, null status, and provenance fields;
flag uncertain terminology for bilingual review.

[Output]
Return one valid English JSON object only, retaining the source record's
incident_id.
