[Input]
Convert the provided standardized Chinese coal mine accident JSON record
into a Chinese instruction-style JSON record.

[Record identifier]
Keep the original incident_id and set task to
safety_recommendation_generation.

[Message structure]
Use messages with exactly three roles: system, user, and assistant.

[System message]
Use a system instruction defining the model as a coal mine safety accident
analysis assistant. The model should analyze the major risks from the
provided accident development process and provide targeted preventive
recommendations without adding accident facts unsupported by the input.

[User message]
Include timeline under Accident Timeline, mechanism under Accident
Mechanism, and root_cause_analysis.contributing_condition under Key
Contributing Factors.

[Assistant message]
Provide 3-6 concise safety recommendations derived from
safety_recommendations, corrective_action, training_action, and
prevention_goal.

[Metadata]
Place all remaining useful accident information under metadata. Do not
repeat timeline, mechanism, contributing_condition, or
safety_recommendations in metadata.

[Personnel]
Rename fatality_person to affected_personnel.

[Source]
Within source, retain agency, source_type, and one official_report URL.

[Grounding]
Do not invent any information.

[Output]
Return valid Chinese JSON only, with no explanation.
