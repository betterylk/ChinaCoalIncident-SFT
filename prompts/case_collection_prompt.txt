[Input and review]
For [Province], inspect all cases listed in [Links file], including
downloadable reports and official webpages. Download available accident
materials and generate/revise standardized JSONL records. Review every
PDF/webpage case by case; do not fabricate accident content using scripts
or templates.

[Source verification]
Retain only cases supported by local source material and an accessible,
verifiable official link, preferably from the National Mine Safety
Administration, its provincial bureaus, or government websites. Verify
that each case page, report, and JSONL record refer to the same mine,
date, and accident.

[File organization]
Move PDFs without a verified official link to [Province]/Unverified
official links/. Store verified PDFs in [Province]/Good/source/ and
corresponding JSONL cases in [Province]/Good/cases/.

[Source grounding]
Base every field strictly on the original report; never infer missing
information. Use "none" or an empty field only when information is not
explicitly available.

[Privacy]
Remove all real personal names and identifiable name forms; replace them
with roles such as "team leader", "worker", "safety inspector", "injured
person", or "on-site personnel".

[Text cleaning]
Remove OCR noise, garbled text, placeholders, "????", headers/footers,
repeated titles, and broken fragments.

[Cross-checking]
Cross-check accident date/time, type, fatalities, mine information,
location, equipment, casualties, and other fields against the original
report.

[overview]
Write 1-3 Chinese sentences summarizing the accident time,
location/worksite, accident type, casualties, and direct economic loss
when explicitly stated.

[mechanism]
Write one concise Chinese paragraph describing the complete sequence: work
performed -> triggering event -> injury/death. Do not copy the report or
merely repeat the accident title.

[timeline]
Construct a complete chronological Chinese timeline from the accident
process, emergency response, reporting, and related sections. Each entry
must follow {"time":"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM","event":"concise complete Chinese
event"}. When available, cover pre-shift meeting/mine entry/worksite
arrival -> work arrangements/key operations -> accident ->
discovery/initial response -> rescue/transportation -> medical
confirmation/reporting. Use only explicitly reported times; preserve
approximate precision and never fabricate minutes.

[root_cause_analysis.]
root_cause: condense the direct cause into a concise and complete
statement.

[contributing_condition]
Condense the principal indirect causes; do not copy entire paragraphs from
the investigation report.

[corrective_action]
Extract concise corrective requirements explicitly supported by the
original report; exclude OCR fragments and boilerplate.

[training_action]
Extract concise training requirements explicitly supported by the original
report; exclude OCR fragments and boilerplate.

[prevention_goal]
Extract concise prevention objectives explicitly supported by the original
report; exclude OCR fragments and boilerplate.

[safety_recommendations]
Extract concise safety recommendations explicitly supported by the
original report; exclude OCR fragments and boilerplate.

[source]
Record the official agency, source type, accessible case_page, and local
report filename/formal report link. Verify every external link
individually.

[Acceptance]
Verify every field in every JSONL record. Each retained case must contain
a non-empty, chronologically ordered timeline that explains the complete
accident process. Do not retain real names, placeholders, "????", garbled
text, large verbatim passages, or invalid, inaccessible, mismatched, or
non-official links.

[Output summary]
Report the number of retained cases and PDFs moved to the unverified
directory. For each retained case, report its filename, accident summary,
timeline-event count, and official link. List cases with insufficient or
unverifiable source material.
